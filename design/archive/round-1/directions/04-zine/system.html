Direction 04Two inks · one gridBrand book

Zine

A risograph zine about building things at home. The paper can be messy; the grid underneath never is.

  1. Two inks, honestly.Every colour is drum 1, drum 2, their overprint, or paper. Nothing else gets printed.
  2. Mess on top, grid underneath.Tape, tilt and notes sit on a 12-column grid and a 4px spacing scale. Break the grid only from the grid.
  3. Handwriting is for asides.Shantell is margin notes and page numbers. Facts are always set in Atkinson.
  4. Jokes in the homelab, not the day job.The day job prints as a flat, full-coverage spread: no stickers, no doodles, no tilt.
  5. Effects never touch reading.Grain sits behind the text, overprint only on display sizes, body ink stays ≥ 7:1.
§ Colourp. 02

Colour

Colour is authored in OKLCH. Each option is a riso ink pairing on a paper stock, implemented as data-option token overrides only. Light stock overprints with mix-blend-mode: multiply; the black stock of nachtdruk uses screen, so light inks brighten where they overlap, like opaque inks on dark board.

    § Typep. 03

    Type

    Display · --font-display

    Bagel Fat One

    A fat, friendly poster face that looks like it came off a drum. One weight, so hierarchy comes from size. Used from step 2 up, never for paragraphs, never all-caps.

    Body · --font-body

    Atkinson Hyperlegible Next

    Variable 200–800, designed for legibility by the Braille Institute. Clear 0/O, 1/l/I. It stays calm next to the noisy display face and holds up at 15px on grain.

    Hand · third slot

    Shantell Sans

    Margin notes and page numbers. Its INFM axis goes from neat to marker and BNCE lifts the baseline. Code uses the system mono stack (ui-monospace), so there is no fourth download.

    § Cover story · Parts list

    Pairing rules

    • Bagel sets every h2 and the cover name; h3 and below are Atkinson 800. Never Bagel under 24px.
    • Labels: Atkinson 700, uppercase, +0.1em tracking. Never track lowercase.
    • Shantell is never longer than two lines and never carries a fact that isn't also in Atkinson nearby.
    • Numbers in specs and stats: tabular-nums slashed-zero.

    § Imagery & rulesp. 10

    Imagery & rules

    Doodles

    • disk
    • host / guest
    • DNS
    • cert
    • radio
    • mini PC
    • warn
    • arrow

    Drawn in one stroke weight (2.2 at 48px), round caps, always currentColor, always aria-hidden. They sit next to a heading that says the same thing. Never in the day-job spread.

    Photos

    Real photos of the setup get a two-ink halftone treatment at build time (duotone, coarse screen). Until they exist, placeholders are CSS dot screens with a PHOTO: label saying what to shoot: the laptop behind the TV, the Zigbee stick on its cable, the wall panel. No stock, no illustrations of people.

    Do / don't

    Tilt a card 0.5–1.1°.Enough to look cut and pasted, not enough to make reading work.
    Ransom-note letters or mixed fonts in a headline.One display face, set straight. The mess lives around the type.
    Overprint display type only.Drum 2 as a screened, misregistered layer on Bagel headings ≥ 24px.
    Overprint, grain or tape over body text.Paragraphs are always flat ink on paper.
    Keep the day job flat and quiet.One title in small caps, responsibilities in plain sentences.
    Stickers, padlocks or jokes on the day job.The humour belongs to the homelab and the clogs.
