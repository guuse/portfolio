§ APrinciplesSYS-A

Principles

  1. 1

    Document, don't decorate.

    Every element has a job a datasheet would recognise: a table, a drawing, a callout, a label. If it doesn't explain something, it goes.

  2. 2

    Numbers are the hero.

    Real values, set in tabular figures with slashed zeros, with units. "10 GB" beats "scalable". A number we can't source doesn't get drawn.

  3. 3

    Ruled, not boxed.

    Hierarchy comes from line weight (2px rule, 1px hairline, dashed for hidden or optional), not from cards and shadows. Plates with a hard offset are kept for diagrams.

  4. 4

    One thing crooked.

    The label tape is the only element allowed to sit off-axis. One per view, carrying the status or the joke. Everything else is on the grid.

  5. 5

    Accent is safety tape.

    The accent marks what to look at: the status, the biggest allocation, the default port. If two things compete for it, one of them loses it.

§ CTypeSYS-C

Type

Archivo for display and body. A grotesque with a real width axis (62–125%). Expanded and heavy, it is the stamped model name on a casing; condensed, it fits a spec table without abbreviating. One family doing both is what makes the page feel engineered.

Martian Mono for labels, part numbers and data. Variable width too (75–112.5%), so labels can be narrow and the tape can be wide. Slashed zero and tabular figures by default.

Not Inter, not Space Grotesk. Two families total, both variable, about 3 WOFF2 files self-hosted.

§ GMotionSYS-G

Motion

Motion is plotting, not performing. Leader lines draw themselves like a pen plotter (--dur-slow, --ease-out), balloons pop in after (--ease-spring), and the status tape gets stuck on once. Nothing loops except the status dot. Under prefers-reduced-motion all of it is off.

§ H–IImagery, do & don'tSYS-H

Iconography and imagery

IconsOnly status shapes: circle-check (ok), triangle (warn), octagon-cross (danger), magnifier (searching). 16px grid, 1.6 stroke, always with a word.
DrawingsOrthographic views, not perspective. Solid 1.6 outline, dashed for hidden parts, chain line for centres, numbered balloons with dot-ended leaders. Mark "not to scale" when it isn't.
PhotosReal setup only: the laptop behind the TV, the Zigbee stick on its cable, the wall panel. Straight-on, daylight, a bit of mess is fine. Until shot, a hatched placeholder saying what to shoot.
NeverBrand logos, product names for the hardware, stock photos, emoji, shields or padlocks, gradient blobs.

Do and don't

Do

“10 GB · real use 2–4 GB” in a ruled row with tabular figures.

Don't

“Scalable infrastructure” on a card with an icon.

Do

One crooked tape per view, carrying the status: Currently: hunting mini PCs

Don't

Tilt the headings, the tables or three tapes next to each other. Crooked only works when everything else is straight.

Do

The title as one plain fact in small type: Tech lead · Infra team

Don't

The title as the big headline, or the areas dressed up as extra job titles.
