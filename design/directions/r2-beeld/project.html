Homelab & home automation

A small home server that runs my apps and my home automation.

Year
2026
Built with
  • Proxmox
  • Kubernetes
  • Home Assistant
  • Zigbee

What it is

An old laptop under the TV runs everything: my side projects, Home Assistant and the lights.

It’s only reachable through my own private network, and every change goes through git, so I can rebuild it in an afternoon. Next up: three small second-hand PCs to turn it into a proper cluster.

Why I built it

I wanted one place for my side projects, and to try things at home before recommending them at work.

How it works

The laptop runs two virtual machines. One runs Kubernetes with my apps; the other runs Home Assistant with a Zigbee stick for the lights and sensors. Nothing is open to the internet; I connect through a private network (Tailscale). When I push code to GitHub, it’s built and deployed automatically (Argo CD).

What I learned

  1. Small mistakes in disk setup add up. Check how much space the installer really gave you.

  2. Keep a simple way in that works when everything else breaks.

  3. Writing everything down makes rebuilding boring, which is exactly what you want.
