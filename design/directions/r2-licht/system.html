Type

Newsreader for headlines and the lead paragraphs, set at regular weight with optical sizing, so the large sizes stay fine instead of heavy. Hanken Grotesk for everything you read and click: warm and slightly round, but quiet. One serif voice for the things Guus says, one sans for the rest.

Spacing & layout

The 4px scale from the foundations. Everything sits on one left edge: a 40rem reading column, and a 72rem wide measure for images, the work columns and the project list. Sections are separated by --space-section and a hairline, not by boxes.

Radius: 3 / 6 / 10px (thumbnail, image, feature). Shadows exist in the tokens but the site uses none; hairlines do the work.

    Motion

    A 520ms fade-and-rise on load, staggered 90ms; the headline underline draws in at 650ms. Hovers are 160ms: rows tint, arrows slide in, the "See projects" arrow bounces once, the chip waves. The cursor preview eases with a lerp. All of it switches off under prefers-reduced-motion.
