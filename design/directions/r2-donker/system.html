System guide

Dark, sleek and plain. One grotesk, a near-black page, one restrained accent, thin dividers and large images. Everything below is the same CSS the pages use (donker.css on top of base.css); the two options only change tokens.

Colour

Ratios are measured (WCAG 2.x relative luminance) from the rendered sRGB values of each OKLCH token. Body ink clears AAA, muted ink clears AAA, subtle ink (labels only) clears AA. Card and divider lines are decorative; interactive outlines (buttons) use --ink-subtle and focus uses the accent, both well above 3:1.

Type

Schibsted Grotesk, one variable family (400–900) for everything. It’s a grotesk drawn for a newspaper group, so it has real character in the details (the tight a, the sharp t, the open G) while staying calm in paragraphs. Headings at 600 with negative tracking; body at 400. No mono: meta uses the same face with tabular numerals, which keeps the page from reading “techy”.

Koppen & the playful bits

Two heading styles, tokens only (data-type on <html>): Strak (a, default) is Schibsted Grotesk 600 with tight tracking; Serif (b) is Instrument Serif 400, a touch larger, with the <em> in italic. Body text stays Schibsted in both. The one recurring wink is the <em> phrase on a slightly tilted accent block that swipes in on load and tips the other way on hover.

Spacing & layout

The shared 4px scale. Sections are separated by --space-section and a hairline; content sits on a 12-column grid with the label in columns 1–4 and the text in 5–11, so every section starts on the same line.

Radius: 4 (tags) · 8 (images, cards) · 14 (feature, hero image). Shadows: none; depth comes from the raised surface and lines.

Motion

Three moves, all short: a staggered rise on load (14px, 760ms, 80ms apart), a slow image scale on hover (520ms, ease-out), and lines that draw in under links. Anchor links scroll smoothly. With prefers-reduced-motion the entrance is off and scrolling jumps.
