Direction 05 · Design system · v1, November 2026

Wandpaneel system sheet

The wall panel of Guus’s house, as a website. A status interface for a person: live readouts first, the long story in detail views below. Numbers are the imagery.

Switch the option in the review bar (bottom left): every swatch, specimen and component on this page re-renders from the same tokens.

01 · Principles

Five rules for the panel

  1. 01Numbers are the imagery.

    Big light numerals, small mono labels. If a tile has no number or state worth reading from across a room, it isn’t a tile.

  2. 02Information decides the span.

    A clock gets width, a SHA gets two columns, a RAM split gets the room it needs to show sixteen cells. Tiles are never all the same size.

  3. 03One hero, one accent.

    The resident tile leads. The accent is the hallway lamp at 20%: it marks the live state and the next thing, nothing else.

  4. 04Readouts first, story second.

    Every tile links into a detail view: rows, logs, settings. The panel is the summary, the views are the case study.

  5. 05Honest about fake data.

    Values are illustrative or derived from the viewer’s clock and say so in one plain line. No tracking, ever.

02 · ColourOKLCH authored · hex for design tools

Colour

Warm plaster and cream for daylight, true black with one vivid lamp-orange for OLED (no glows, no shadows), and pure greyscale for e-ink, where meters switch from flat fills to CSS dither patterns and status is carried by shape and word only.

    03 · TypeOnest + Martian Mono

    Type

    Display & body · Onest, variable 200–800

    10:24 283 0

    A grotesk with warm, slightly rounded numerals (the flagged 1, the open 4) that stay readable at thermostat sizes in a light weight, and a true tnum feature for anything that ticks. It reads as a product UI without being Inter, and it isn’t on the banned list.

    Data & labels · Martian Mono, wdth 75–112.5

    ARGO CD 16/16 · a41c9e2 · 0

    A wide-set mono with a width axis. Set at 87.5% width and +0.07em tracking in caps it becomes the panel’s label voice: appliance-like, dense, unmistakably not JetBrains Mono. Slashed zero is on.

    Pairing rules

    2 families total

    Numbers
    Onest, weight --c-num-weight (300 daylight, 250 OLED, 400 e-ink), tracking −0.035 to −0.05em. Units drop to 42% size in --ink-muted.
    Tabular
    tabular-nums slashed-zero on everything that changes faster than once a minute: seconds, countdowns, RAM use, logs, specs. The clock’s HH:MM uses proportional figures on purpose: it changes once a minute and Onest’s tabular 1 opens an ugly gap at 100px.
    Labels
    Martian Mono, caps, 87.5% width, +0.07em. Never for sentences longer than a line.
    Title
    One title, “Tech lead · Infra team”, only ever as a label-sized fact. Security, cloud and architecture are areas the job covers, never titles.

    06 · MotionMotion explains, it doesn’t decorate

    Motion

    Tokens · press play

    --dur-instant 80ms · toggles, press

    --dur-quick 160ms · hover, chevrons (= --c-dur)

    --dur-base 280ms · ease-in-out

    --dur-slow 520ms · RAM use meter drift

    Live behaviour

    LiveThe only loop on the site. Off under reduced motion and on e-ink.

    Clock
    Ticks per second; e-ink hides seconds (--c-seconds: none), like a real panel refreshing per minute.
    E-ink
    --c-dur: 0ms: nothing tweens on paper. Switching to e-ink plays a 300ms black/white refresh flash, skipped under reduced motion.
    Reduced
    No flash, no pulse, no meter drift, instant scroll.
    07 · Iconography & imagery

    Glyphs, not icons

    Six drawn glyphs, 1.5px stroke, square caps, only where a shape carries meaning a word can’t carry as fast. No icon set, no emoji, no logos. Photography is the real setup; until it’s shot, placeholders say exactly what goes there.
