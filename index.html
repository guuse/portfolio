§4 To decide before the build

  • Pick a direction (or a mix) and an option. Leave dark mode to prefers-color-scheme, or offer a manual switch?
  • Confirm the [CONFIRM] items in CONTENT.md: since when, the security framework(s), which clients may be named, Quizmaster's stack, contact address.
  • Pick the hero one-liner: A ("secure by day, a laptop behind the TV by night"), B ("fifteen services, lid shut") or C ("how it's built, where it runs, who gets in").
  • Real home automations and devices for §4.7, to replace the placeholders.
  • Photos: the laptop behind the TV, the Zigbee stick on its extension cable, the wall panel, and one of you.
  • Is huisjes (the house hunt) public-OK, or does it stay off the site?
  • Hosting: Cloudflare Pages on the apex (recommended) or self-hosted through the tunnel.
